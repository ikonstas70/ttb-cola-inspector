⚖️ 27 CFR Part 16 — Government Health Warning Statement Requirements

The health warning statement is mandatory on all alcoholic beverages sold or distributed in the United States containing not less than 0.5% alcohol by volume.

GOVERNMENT WARNING: (1) According to the Surgeon General, women should not drink alcoholic beverages during pregnancy because of the risk of birth defects. (2) Consumption of alcoholic beverages impairs your ability to drive a car or operate machinery, and may cause health problems.

Key Compliance Rules:

  • Mandatory All-Caps Header: The words GOVERNMENT WARNING: must appear in all capital letters and bold print.
  • Exact Verbatim Text: No omissions, abbreviations, or wording modifications are permitted under federal law.
  • Conspicuous Placement: Must appear on a contrasting background, clearly separated from other label copy.

🍷 Beverage Category Standards of Identity

  • Distilled Spirits (27 CFR Part 5): Brand Name, Class/Type Designation, ABV & Proof (Proof must mathematically equal $2 \times \text{ABV}$), Net Contents (Standard metric sizes: 50 mL, 100 mL, 200 mL, 375 mL, 750 mL, 1 L, 1.75 L), Bottler Name/Address, Country of Origin (if imported).
  • Wine (27 CFR Part 4): Brand Name, Class/Type (e.g. Cabernet Sauvignon), Appellation of Origin (if required), ABV (mandatory if over 14% ABV; "Table Wine" permitted for 7–14%), Net Contents, Bottler declaration.
  • Malt Beverages / Beer (27 CFR Part 7): Brand Name, Class/Type (e.g. India Pale Ale, Stout), Net Contents, Name and Address of Brewer/Packer, Alcohol Content (where required by state law).
